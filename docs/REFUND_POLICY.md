# PocketSentinel Refund & Billing Policy

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026  
**Policy Version:** 2.0  
**Core Model:** 0% Negotiation Rake, Free Open-Source Core, 14-Day Hosted Guarantee  

---

## 1. Our Core Principle: 0% Negotiation Rake

Legacy subscription cancellation and bill negotiation applications typically charge users between 30% and 50% of any "savings" they claim to discover, extracting substantial cuts from household budgets.

**PocketSentinel takes a 0% rake.**
* Every dollar, euro, or unit of currency saved or refunded through our automated dispute logs, cancellation scripts, or merchant escalation workflows belongs entirely to you.
* We do not take success fees, negotiation cuts, or contingency percentages.

---

## 2. Free Open-Source Core Platform

The core PocketSentinel client, local Express daemon, WebCrypto cryptographic vault, and Sui/Walrus memory inspector are 100% free open-source software distributed under the MIT License.
* **No Software Purchase Fees:** There are no initial purchase fees, licensing charges, or mandatory recurring dues to use the self-hosted software.
* **Direct Third-Party Billing:** If you provide your own API keys for services such as Groq, Google Gemini, or Tatum, billing is handled directly between you and those third-party providers in accordance with their respective terms. PocketSentinel receives no commission or payment from your direct API usage.

---

## 3. Hosted Cloud Services and API Relayer Subscriptions

If you subscribe to an optional managed PocketSentinel hosted cloud tier (e.g., hosted LPU copilot relays, cloud receipt OCR processing, or multi-device cryptographic sync):

1. **14-Day Money-Back Guarantee:** We offer a full, no-questions-asked refund within fourteen (14) calendar days of your initial subscription payment or plan upgrade.
2. **Cancellation Anytime:** You may cancel recurring hosted subscriptions at any time via your account settings. Upon cancellation, your access remains active until the end of your current billing period, after which no further charges will occur.
3. **Prorated Credits:** We do not issue partial or prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations after the initial 14-day window has expired; however, you will retain full service access through the remainder of the paid term.

---

## 4. Blockchain Gas Fees and Onchain Storage

PocketSentinel enables verification of price baselines and trial horizons on the Sui Mainnet and Walrus Memory Protocol:
* **Non-Refundable Gas Costs:** Network gas fees paid to Sui network validators or decentralized storage providers (Mysten Labs / Walrus) are paid directly to decentralized blockchain validators. These transactions are mathematically irreversible and permanently committed to the ledger.
* **No Custody of Crypto Assets:** PocketSentinel does not hold custody of your digital assets or private keys, and cannot reverse onchain transactions or refund blockchain network gas fees.

---

## 5. Refund Request Workflow

To request a refund for an active hosted cloud subscription within the 14-day guarantee window:
1. Contact our billing support team at **billing@pocketsentinel.app** or open a ticket through the GitHub issue tracker.
2. Include your registered email address, transaction reference, and date of purchase.
3. Refunds are reviewed and processed within three (3) to five (5) business days and returned to your original payment method.

---

## 6. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you believe an unauthorized charge has occurred on your card for a PocketSentinel hosted service, please contact us immediately at **billing@pocketsentinel.app**. We are committed to resolving billing discrepancies promptly without the need for lengthy financial intermediary disputes.
